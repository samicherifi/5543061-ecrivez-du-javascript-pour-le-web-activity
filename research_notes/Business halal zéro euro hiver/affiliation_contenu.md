# Zero-budget content-based affiliate marketing (faceless YouTube, SEO niche site, Pinterest, newsletter): state of play in October 2026 for a French beginner

Scope note: I gathered this in about 17 tool calls, so coverage is uneven. Official pages were verified for YouTube policy, Systeme.io, ElevenLabs, Make, Hostinger (via Hostinger's own article) and beehiiv. Most of the other numbers come from secondary blogs or podcast descriptions and are labelled as such. Anything I did not verify is listed under Gaps.

## 1. What do recognized experts recommend in 2025-2026 (Income School, Pat Flynn, Niche Pursuits, Les Wizards, etc.)?

### Takeaway
The experts I could check have moved away from "pure Google SEO niche site" toward several channels at once: YouTube, email, short video and private affiliate deals, built on content that shows real first-hand experience. The French Wizards still teach a 0 → 1,500 €/month first-year affiliate roadmap that combines SEO and YouTube. I found no verified, recent beginner income figures from any of these experts. Their published numbers are either targets or survivor stories.

### Cited Findings
- **Income School / Project 24.** Project 24 is described as "our membership program that teaches you how to work toward replacing your current income with income from websites and YouTube channels in 24 months". Income School's own materials say the results shown are "not typical". The 24-month horizon is their own framing. — [Income School](https://incomeschool.com/?p=75524); [Income School video page](https://incomeschool.com/?p=73104)
- Income School has produced content on Google's helpful-content self-assessment questions, including using ChatGPT to turn Google's questions into content checklists. It has also run live sessions on Google's AI search and "the future of blogging". — [Income School: Let's talk Google, SEO and the future of blogging](https://incomeschool.com/video-post/live-lets-talk-google-seo-and-the-future-of-blogging/); [Income School: SEO and AI shifts](https://incomeschool.com/video-post/seo-and-ai-shifts-bravery-breeds-success/)
- **Niche Pursuits (Spencer Haws's media), May 2026 podcast.** Food blogger Carrie Forrest went from about 1M to about 200k monthly pageviews (−80%) after Google updates and AI Overviews. Reported ad income fell from $20–30k/month to $4–5k/month, and Amazon affiliate income fell from about $35k/year to $150–200/month. Her rebuild relies on email, short-form video and private affiliates. — [Niche Pursuits](https://www.nichepursuits.com/?p=142573); [CastFox episode summary](https://www.castfox.net/podcast/niche-pursuits-podcast-find-your-next-niche-business-idea-315097/episode/how-carrie-forrest-lost-80-of-traffic-and-rebuilt-her-blog-1491580) (figures come from the episode summary, not a transcript)
- **Pat Flynn (Smart Passive Income), 14 Oct 2025 newsletter.** Affiliate commissions made up "over half" of his revenue in his first decade, and they came "naturally" from recommending tools he had actually used. — [SPI newsletter](https://cksn.smartpassiveincome.com/posts/the-revenue-stream-you-re-probably-ignoring-but-shouldn-t)
- Pat Flynn is credited with more than $100k in commissions from one evergreen Descript tutorial video. Note that this came from a channel that was already large. — [Goodreads blog repost](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/25834835-he-earned-100k-from-this-1-review-video); [Mango Network Substack](https://themangonetwork.substack.com/p/affiliate-marketing-success-how-pat) (secondary sources)
- **Les Wizards (Anthony, Arthur, Franck), episode "AFFILIATION : De 0 à 1500€ par mois", July 2024 (older data).** Their method runs: niche choice (a slice of a big market vs a small market) → launch fast on WordPress (Easy Hoster, theme, plugins) → become the reference on one topic, with content briefs and no filler → netlinking (media links, link-selling platforms) → conversion (sticky CTAs, clickable boxes) → YouTube as an evergreen affiliate channel → patience. One chapter is titled "Ne pas abuser de l'IA" ("don't overuse AI"). The page gives no actual income figures, only the 1,500 €/month target. — [Wizards Podcast on Ausha](https://podcast.ausha.co/wizards-podcast/affiliation-de-0-a-1500-par-mois-guide-complet-etape-par-etape)
- Other Wizards episodes cover "borderline" affiliation, parasite SEO, a site built around AI detectors, and YouTube in hard niches. They also recommend paid link vendors (Linkuma, Shinobi) with promo codes, which are probably affiliate links, so the advice is not neutral. This approach also conflicts with a 0 € budget. — [Wizards: affiliation borderline](https://podcast.ausha.co/wizards-podcast/affiliation-borderline-etude-de-cas-site-affiliation-avenir-du-seo); [Wizards: niches les plus dures](https://podcast.ausha.co/wizards-podcast/la-verite-sur-les-niches-d-affiliation-seo-les-plus-dures-au-monde-espionnage-secrets-et-pire)
- Wizards Academy is the paid course of the three hosts. — [WPMarmite](https://wpmarmite.com/formationwp/wizards-academy-la-formation-complete-en-affiliation/)

### Inferences
- Every expert I found sells a course or a membership (Project 24, Wizards Academy, SPI). Their income claims are marketing for that product, and none publishes the median outcome for students.
- For a 0 € beginner, the experts converge on four points: pick one narrow topic, publish consistently for 12–24 months, use YouTube and email as protection against Google volatility, and only promote tools you have actually used.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve current (2025-2026) statements from Ali Abdaal, Matt Par / Tube Mastery, the MVP podcast (SEO/GEO) or Olivier Roland, so no quotes from them are included.
- I did not verify whether Jim Harmer is still part of Income School.
- I found no published 2025-2026 Niche Pursuits niche-site income reports for new sites.

## 2. YouTube vs SEO blog vs Pinterest vs newsletter in 2026: which reaches a first commission fastest?

### Takeaway
No independent study measures time to first commission by channel. All the timelines below are estimates from vendors or blogs. SEO is now the slowest and riskiest channel for a newcomer. YouTube (searchable tutorials and reviews of tools) and Pinterest (direct affiliate links allowed) can produce a first commission within roughly 1–4 months. A newsletter converts best but needs a traffic source first.

### Cited Findings
- **SEO/Google risk.** One study of 7,105 niche/affiliate sites found that nearly 50% lost more than 90% of organic traffic between Dec 2023 and Aug 2024. — [Digistore24 blog](https://www.digistore24.com/blog/affiliate-marketing-fails/) (secondary; the original study is not linked)
- Google search referrals to publishers fell 33% globally in 2025. Affiverse reports that 71% of affiliate sites lost rankings in the March 2026 core update, and HouseFresh lost about 91–95% of its Google traffic over 18 months. — [GetChatAds](https://www.getchatads.com/blog/affiliate-blog-ai-chat-vs-new-posts/) (an aggregator; the primary Chartbeat and Affiverse data was not verified)
- A UK blogger who earned about £10k/month at peak in 2022 reports a decline caused by Google and AI. He claims that being cited inside an AI Overview raises CTR by about 35% compared with ranking below it. — [Household Money Saving](https://www.householdmoneysaving.com/how-much-does-a-blogger-make/) (single-blogger claim)
- **Pinterest.** A vendor estimates a first sale in 1–3 months for active pinners, the first $100 in months 2–4, and $1k+/month after 6–12 months. It recommends 20–50% affiliate pins and requires a disclosure (#ad). Pinterest has allowed direct affiliate links since 2017. — [Unilink](https://www.unilink.us/blog/pinterest-affiliate-marketing-2026) (vendor; optimistic)
- A more conservative source says most beginners earn little or nothing for 6–12 months, with $0–100/month in the first 6 months. — [BloggersPassion](https://bloggerspassion.com/affiliate-marketing-for-beginners/)
- **YouTube.** A 2026 guide estimates 500–800 subscribers and $0–50/month in months 4–6 for a new channel. — [Kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/how-much-faceless-youtube-channels-make/) (estimate, not data)
- Affiliate links work on YouTube from video #1. They do not depend on YPP (YouTube Partner Program) eligibility, which requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 watch hours or 10M Shorts views in 90 days. — [BloggersIdeas](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2162249)
- **Newsletter.** beehiiv pays 50% for 12 months on referrals (details in section 4). I found no data on how long it takes to earn a first affiliate payment. — [beehiiv support](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/23352219299095-Getting-started-with-the-beehiiv-Partner-Program)
- Niche Pursuits' rebuild case points to email plus short video plus private affiliates as the resilient combination. — [CastFox summary](https://www.castfox.net/podcast/niche-pursuits-podcast-find-your-next-niche-business-idea-315097/episode/how-carrie-forrest-lost-80-of-traffic-and-rebuilt-her-blog-1491580)

### Inferences
- **Fastest realistic route for a 0 € French beginner:** French-language YouTube tutorials on SaaS/AI tools (high purchase intent, searchable, affiliate links in the description from day 1), plus a free newsletter that captures viewers, plus Pinterest as a secondary channel if the niche is visual. An SEO site is best treated as a slow long-term asset, or used only as a hub for the newsletter and links.
- **Realistic ranges** (my synthesis of the estimates above, not measured data):
  - **Month 3:** 0 € is the most likely outcome; 0–50 € if things go well.
  - **Month 6:** 0–200 €/month.
  - **Month 12:** 0–1,000 €/month. The Wizards target of 1,500 € is an upper-quartile goal, not a median.
  - A large share of beginners make 0 € in their first year (see the 6–12 month "little or nothing" estimate above).
- **Passivity.** Recurring SaaS commissions plus evergreen video can become semi-passive after 12+ months. During year 1 the model is full-time active work.

### Gaps
- No independent data on time to first commission broken down by channel.
- No French-market-specific data (French YouTube RPMs, French Pinterest usage).

## 3. Faceless YouTube: case studies, the July 2025 "inauthentic content" policy, and how to stay compliant

### Takeaway
On 15 July 2025, YouTube renamed its "repetitious content" policy to "inauthentic content". Mass-produced, generic, template-based AI content cannot be monetized. AI used as a tool (scripts, visuals) is allowed when the result adds original value. The faceless case studies with income figures are all self-reported and unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **Official YouTube monetization policy.** Changes on 15 July 2025: "We are also renaming this policy from 'repetitious content' to 'inauthentic content.'" Monetized content must "Not be mass-produced, generic, repetitive, or manipulative." The policy lists "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production" as not allowed. It treats using AI to edit scripts or to generate a unique background visual as acceptable. "There is no change to our reused content policy." A separate rule covers AI-generated personas that deliver information on sensitive topics. — [YouTube Help: YPP monetization policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- YPP lead Rene Ritchie called it a "minor update". Commentators note that the rules are not new but enforcement is stricter. Changing speed, adding music or cropping does not make reused content original. Highly templated Shorts are a focus. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/youtube-updates-monetisation-policies-ai-and-repetitive-content-ban-begins-july-15-1.500192660); [Plagiarism Today](https://www.plagiarismtoday.com/2025/07/08/youtube-targets-inauthentic-content/); [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-clarifies-monetisation-policy-targeting-inauthentic-content-9493155)
- **Self-reported case studies (unverified):**
  - A rental-property channel reached 28k subscribers in 6 months, with about $2,800/month AdSense plus $1,500/month affiliate income at month 7. — [Kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/how-much-faceless-youtube-channels-make/)
  - A channel with 1.9M monthly views and about $11.5k revenue, which works out to roughly a $6 RPM (undated). — [Kineclip](https://kineclip.com/blog/how-much-faceless-youtube-channels-make/)
- **RPM by niche:** finance $12–25 (excluded on halal grounds); horror/stories $4–8. — [Eliro](https://eliro.pro/blog/best-faceless-youtube-niches-pay-most); [BloggersIdeas](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2162249) (ranges vary widely between sources)

### Inferences
Compliance checklist derived from the policy text:
- Each video should add real value: an original script with the human's own testing and screen recordings of the tool actually being used.
- No re-uploads of other people's footage.
- Vary formats instead of using one template.
- Use a consistent voice (a real voice, or a clearly disclosed synthetic one).
- Avoid AI personas on sensitive topics.
- Use YouTube's altered/synthetic-content disclosure where it applies.

Other points:
- "Faceless tool tutorials", meaning screen recordings plus voiceover on Canva, Systeme.io or Make, fit the policy better than fully generated AI slideshow channels. They also match the Pat Flynn/Descript model.
- Ad revenue is unlikely before YPP eligibility. In the first 6–12 months, affiliate income is the main lever.

### Gaps
- No verified French-language faceless channel case study.
- No verified RPM for French-language audiences.
- I did not fetch the YouTube altered/synthetic content disclosure page itself.

## 4. Halal-compatible affiliate programs accessible to French beginners (recurring SaaS/AI tools)

### Takeaway
Verified on official pages:
- **Systeme.io:** 60% lifetime recurring (French company, very beginner-friendly).
- **Make:** 35% for 12 months.
- **ElevenLabs:** 22% for 12 months.
- **beehiiv:** 50% for 12 months.
- **Hostinger:** 40% on hosting, one-time.

Notion appears closed to new affiliates in mid-2026, and the Canva rate could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- **Systeme.io (official page, current as of April 2026):**
  - 60% "on every sale", recurring for the life of the customer.
  - Lifetime attribution ("tagged to you permanently").
  - PayPal or wire payout, $30 minimum, paid monthly on the 10th.
  - Older reviews say 40% (and a 5% second tier), so those figures are outdated or conflicting.
  - [Systeme.io affiliate program](https://systeme.io/affiliate-program); conflicting: [SchoolMaker review](https://www.schoolmaker.com/blog/systeme-io-affiliate-program), [Craig Campbell](https://craigcampbellseo.com/systeme-io-affiliate-program/)
- **Make (official help center):**
  - 35% on referrals for 12 months, counted from the referral's registration date rather than first payment.
  - Applies to subscriptions only, not extra operations.
  - Payout via Wise from $100 and 3 paying users.
  - Some landing-page copy still says 24 months, which appears stale.
  - [Make Help: Affiliate program](https://help.make.com/affiliate-program?x=3)
- **ElevenLabs (official):**
  - 22% for the first 12 months on Starter/Creator/Pro/Scale plans; 11% on Business; 0% on Enterprise.
  - 90-day cookie.
  - Paid via PartnerStack with a $5 minimum, only after the license has been active for more than 90 days. Refunds are clawed back.
  - [ElevenLabs affiliates](https://elevenlabs.io/affiliate)
- **Hostinger:**
  - 40% on web hosting sales (one-time, not recurring), 30-day cookie.
  - Hostinger's own article does not show payout terms. The thresholds below come only from a third-party review: $100 minimum via PayPal, or $500 via bank transfer plus 3 approved sales; commissions released after 45 days. Third-party reviews also claim 40–60% (unconfirmed).
  - [Hostinger tutorial (own site)](https://www.hostinger.com/tutorials/affiliate-marketing-programs/.); payout terms and 40–60% claim: [BloggersPassion review](https://bloggerspassion.com/hostinger-affiliate/)
- **beehiiv (official support, updated March 2026):**
  - 50% for 12 months, 60-day first-touch cookie.
  - Example given: a Scale plan referral pays $49.50/month for a year.
  - Third-party sources mention tiers up to 60% and a $15k cap per referral, which conflicts with beehiiv's blog saying there is no cap.
  - PayPal payout.
  - [beehiiv support](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/23352219299095-Getting-started-with-the-beehiiv-Partner-Program); [beehiiv blog](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/beehiiv-partner-program-promotion); [Reditus](https://getreditus.com/affiliate-programs/beehiiv)
- **Notion:**
  - Older reviews say 50%; newer sources say up to $50 per activated signup plus 20% of first-year revenue via PartnerStack.
  - Reportedly not accepting new affiliates as of mid-2026.
  - [Reditus: Notion](https://getreditus.com/affiliate-programs/notion); [NotionApps 2025](https://www.notionapps.com/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-notion-affiliate-program-2025/)
- **Canva:**
  - Runs through the Canvassador program. The commonly cited ~$36 per annual Pro referral is not verified from canva.com.
  - [Referly: Canva](https://www.referly.so/affiliate-programs/canva); [AdSpyder](https://adspyder.io/blog/canva-affiliate-program/)

### Inferences
- **Halal screening.** These are software subscriptions: marketing, automation, voice AI, hosting, newsletters. They are not interest-, gambling- or alcohol-based, so they look permissible in principle. Two caveats: the person should avoid promoting them for use cases that are themselves impermissible, and should check that payouts are not tied to interest-bearing arrangements. A scholar's confirmation is advisable; this is not a fatwa.
- **Best fit for a French beginner:** Systeme.io is French-founded, has a free plan, pays 60% lifetime and has a low $30 threshold. It is the strongest anchor offer.
- The 12-month programs (Make, ElevenLabs, beehiiv) are "recurring" only for one year.
- **Payout friction** delays the first euro actually received even after a sale:
  - ElevenLabs waits more than 90 days after the sale.
  - Make requires $100 and 3 paying users.
  - Hostinger has a $100 threshold and a 45-day hold.

### Gaps
- Canva and Notion official 2026 terms were not verified.
- I did not check Amazon.fr Partenaires or French programs (Awin, Affilae) for halal-compatible physical products.
- The Remotion license terms (see section 5) were not retrieved.

## 5. What Claude / Claude Code can automate vs what the human must do

### Takeaway
Claude and Claude Code can produce most of the "production" layer: research, scripts, code, site generation and pipelines. The human has to own the parts that platform policies and trust require: real first-hand testing of the tools, voice or final review, account creation and identity/tax setup, editorial judgment, and deciding what gets published. YouTube's policy explicitly allows AI as an editing aid but refuses to monetize mass-produced AI templates ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)).

### Cited Findings
- YouTube allows "using AI to edit your video scripts or generate a unique background visual". It does not allow "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production". — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)
- Income School demonstrates using an LLM (ChatGPT) to turn Google's helpful-content questions into content checks. — [Income School](https://incomeschool.com/?p=73104)
- Les Wizards warn against overusing AI in affiliate content (chapter "Ne pas abuser de l'IA"). — [Wizards Podcast](https://podcast.ausha.co/wizards-podcast/affiliation-de-0-a-1500-par-mois-guide-complet-etape-par-etape)

### Inferences
This split is my own analysis, based on tool capabilities and not on an external source.

**Claude / Claude Code can do:**
- Keyword and topic clustering from free data (Google autocomplete, YouTube search suggestions, "People Also Ask" scraping scripts).
- Content briefs, script drafts in French, titles and descriptions with affiliate disclosure, chapters.
- Building a static site (Astro/Hugo/Next) with free hosting (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages) and automated publishing through GitHub Actions.
- Pinterest pin images generated as code (HTML/SVG → PNG).
- Programmatic video assembly with Remotion: screen recordings plus voiceover plus captions plus motion graphics.
- Newsletter drafts.
- Tracking scripts (UTM links, simple dashboards).

**The human must do:**
- Use and test the products: screen recordings and real opinions are what make the content "authentic".
- Record or approve the voice.
- Create the accounts (YouTube, Pinterest, affiliate programs, beehiiv/Systeme.io) and pass KYC.
- Handle French administrative registration for income (micro-entreprise declaration).
- Make the final factual and halal review, and reply to comments and emails.

**Risk:** a fully automated, high-volume pipeline is exactly the pattern YouTube's "inauthentic content" policy targets, and the pattern Google's helpful-content system penalizes. Automation should raise quality per video, not volume.

### Gaps
- Remotion licensing for individuals (free tier conditions) was not verified; I could not retrieve the license page.
- No primary-source case study of a Claude Code–driven affiliate pipeline with income data was found.
- French micro-entreprise rules for affiliate income are outside this research's scope.
