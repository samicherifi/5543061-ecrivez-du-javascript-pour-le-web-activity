# Zero-budget digital products and print-on-demand (POD) for a French beginner (Oct 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-15. Researcher note: about 17 tool calls. Most fee figures come from third-party blogs. Gumroad's figures were checked on its official pricing page and Redbubble's on its official blog. Every figure below should be checked again on the platform's own page before acting.

## 1. Startup costs, fees, payout and VAT/TVA handling: which platforms really allow a 0 € start from France?

### Takeaway
Gumroad (0 € upfront, merchant of record (MoR) since 1 Jan 2025) and Payhip free plan (0 € upfront, 5 % fee, EU and UK VAT handled) are the cleanest 0 € options for a French seller. Etsy is almost free: $0.20 per listing, a possible one-time setup fee, and about 11 % or more in fees per sale for France. POD through Printify or Printful connected to Etsy costs nothing upfront, because production is paid only after a customer pays. Lemon Squeezy is in transition to Stripe Managed Payments and is not a safe default for a new seller in 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Gumroad**
- Official pricing page: 10 % + $0.50 per transaction on direct sales, a flat 30 % on sales through the Gumroad "Discover" marketplace, and no monthly fee — [Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing)
- Official: since 1 Jan 2025 Gumroad collects and remits sales tax and VAT worldwide (it acts as MoR). Sellers do not manage tax settings. Payouts go by direct deposit or PayPal, and the method "varies by country". The page states no minimum payout — [Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing)
- Third-party reports that payment processing (about 2.9 % + $0.30, or 3.9 % internationally on Stripe) comes on top of the 10 %, for an effective take of about 13–21 %. Fees are not refunded when a sale is refunded — [Checkoutpage](https://checkoutpage.com/blog/gumroad-fees); [Chargepanda](https://www.chargepanda.com/blog/post/why-self-hosted-beats-gumroad-for-serious-creators). This partly conflicts with the official page, which lists no separate processing fee. Gumroad has said the 10 % + $0.50 now includes processing, but I could not verify that wording on the page.

**Payhip**
- Free plan: 0 €/month and 5 % per sale. Plus plan: $29/month at 2 %. Pro plan: $99/month at 0 %. PayPal or Stripe processing (about 2.9 % + $0.30) comes on top — [Fungies Payhip review 2026](https://fungies.io/payhip-review-2026); [Dodo Payments review](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/payhip-review). Both are competitor blogs.
- MoR status is disputed. Some reviews say Payhip collects and remits EU and UK digital VAT on every plan but is not a full MoR for US, Canadian or Australian tax. Others call it an MoR — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/sellfy-vs-payhip-vs-gumroad-2026); [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/payhip-review). For a France-based seller selling mostly to the EU, the EU VAT coverage is what matters.

**Lemon Squeezy**
- Lemon Squeezy was acquired by Stripe. In Jan–Feb 2026 it announced a migration toward "Stripe Managed Payments" (Stripe as MoR handling VAT, GST and sales tax), which entered Stripe's API around April 2026. The reported fee is 5 % + $0.50. Managed Payments reportedly lacks Lemon Squeezy's digital-download delivery, affiliates and storefront — [Fungies](https://fungies.io/lemon-squeezy-stripe-acquisition-saas-founders-2026/); [Dodo Payments](https://dodopayments.com/blogs/lemon-squeezy-vs-stripe/) (both are competitor blogs). Whether a new seller can sign up today is unverified.

**Etsy (France)**
- Fees reported for France: $0.20 listing fee (a listing lasts 4 months and the fee is charged again on auto-renew after a sale), a 6.5 % transaction fee on the total paid, payment processing of 4 % + €0.30, a France "regulatory operating fee" of about 0.47 % (2026), Offsite Ads of 12–15 % on attributed sales, and 2.5 % currency conversion if the shop currency differs from the bank currency — [Crosslist](https://crosslist.com/blog/etsy-review); [Growing Your Craft](https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/how-much-does-it-really-cost-to-sell-on-etsy); [Craftybase calculator](https://craftybase.com/etsy-fee-calculator)
- A one-time shop setup fee of about $15–29 is reported for new shops in some countries. No France-specific amount was found — [Crosslist](https://crosslist.com/blog/etsy-review)

**POD platforms**
- Printful: free plan with unlimited stores and all products. The paid Growth plan ($24.99/month) only lowers product costs. Reported t-shirt base cost is $9.25–13.50 plus shipping, and prices rose on 26 Feb 2026 for some Cotton Heritage products — [eCommerceCEO](https://ecommerceceo.com/printful-pricing/); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/209769/Printful/pricing/)
- Printify: free plan with up to 5 stores and unlimited designs. Premium is about $29–39/month for about 20 % off products (sources conflict). It has two-way sync with Etsy — [Costbench](https://costbench.com/software/ecommerce/printify/free-plan) (contains errors, so treat with caution); [Listify](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/printful-vs-printify-etsy-sellers-2026)
- Redbubble (official blog, Aug 2025): introduced account fees and an "excess markup fee", capped at $150/month. Pro accounts are exempt — [Redbubble blog](https://blog.redbubble.com/2025/08/excess-markup-fee-explained/). A third-party guide gives a 50 % platform fee for Standard accounts and 20 % for Premium (unverified) — [Autokeyworder](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/how-to-sell-on-redbubble)
- TeePublic (owned by Redbubble): one blog says it has no platform fee but hides designs from new accounts in search. No official 2025–26 fee notice was found — [Autokeyworder](https://autokeyworder.com/blog/teepublic-vs-redbubble). Spreadshirt: reported to charge 20 % on shop sales (undated, possibly old) — [Moneypantry](https://moneypantry.com/sites-like-redbubble/)

**French legal and tax context**
- DAC7: platforms such as Etsy report sellers to the tax authority above 2 000 € OR 30 sales per year, including identity, IBAN and turnover — [Margeo](https://margeoapp.com/blog/seuil-3000-euros-plateformes-declaration) (vendor blog)
- Micro-entreprise: one table gives contributions for goods sales of 12.3 % of turnover (9.3 % with ACRE) and an optional 1 % versement libératoire (flat-rate income tax). The ceilings quoted conflict (203,100 € vs 188,700 €) — [Ateliers d'Art PDF](https://www.ateliersdart.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Tableau_comparatif_des_statuts_2024.pdf) (2024, older). How downloadable files are classified (BIC or BNC) was not resolved.

### Inferences
- Gumroad and Payhip (free) need no money upfront at all. On Etsy, around 10–20 listings cost about $2–4, plus a possible setup fee. Strictly speaking that is not 0 €, but Etsy is the only option here that brings its own buyer traffic.
- A redesigned POD option on Etsy needs no stock and no upfront cost: the customer pays Etsy first, then Printify or Printful charges the seller's card. The seller still needs a bank card with a small float. Margins are thin (a $25 tee minus about $12–15 base and shipping, minus about 11 % Etsy fees).
- Selling regularly from France legally requires micro-entrepreneur registration (free to create). Gumroad as MoR, or Payhip, removes the EU VAT OSS burden, because the micro-entrepreneur "franchise en base" does not cover cross-border B2C digital sales once they pass the EU 10,000 € threshold.
- Redbubble's 2025 fee changes make it unattractive for a beginner with few sales.

### Gaps
- No official confirmation of the Etsy France setup fee amount in 2026, or of Payhip's exact MoR scope.
- Gumroad payout minimum and timing for France were not verified.
- Whether Lemon Squeezy or Stripe Managed Payments accepts new French individuals in Oct 2026 is not confirmed.
- How French tax treats digital downloads (BIC sales or BNC) is unconfirmed. An accountant or URSSAF should be checked.

## 2. Realistic sales data, time to first sale, income at 3/6/12 months: what experts and data say

### Takeaway
The headline success stories (Thomas Frank, Easlo, Ali Abdaal, Justin Welsh) all rest on a large audience built beforehand. They are not representative of beginners. Data for beginner Etsy digital sellers points to a first sale after 30–60 days, about 0–50 €/month in months 1–2, and maybe 100–250 €/month (median shop) after several months with dozens of listings. Rare outliers reach about $800–2,000/month after 3–12 months.

### Cited Findings
- **Thomas Frank** (self-reported, verified primary source): about $2.1M in sales of two Notion templates over about 2 years, about $120k/month in template sales at the time, with traffic from a large YouTube channel — [Thomas Frank on Typefully](https://typefully.com/TomFrankly/dollar1-million-in-notion-template-sales-kuFT0iD). A marketing blog claims over $2.5M (conflicting, unverified) — [Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/blog/can-you-make-money-selling-notion-templates)
- **Easlo** (third-party claims, unverified): over $500k from Notion templates, "$20k/month by Oct 2022", and elsewhere "$10k/month" — [Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/blog/can-you-make-money-selling-notion-templates); [SideNerds](https://sidenerds.beehiiv.com/p/notion-templates). Older data (2022–2024).
- **Ali Abdaal** (summary of his 2022 income video): about $4.6M revenue and about $2.07M profit, about $1.76M of it from Part-Time YouTuber Academy and other cohort courses — [Summify](https://summify.io/discover/how-much-money-i-made-as-a-youtuber-entrepreneur-2022-KvDHMe/). This is 2022 data, from an audience of millions.
- **Justin Welsh** (self-reported, figures inconsistent across sources): over $12.5M lifetime at about 86 % margin per one case study, $7M+ per another, and about $1.7M in 2023. His LinkedIn course sells at $99 — [Built by Foundry](https://www.builtbyfoundry.io/blog/justin-welsh-solopreneur-12m-empire); [My First 10K](https://myfirst10k.beehiiv.com/p/justin-welsh-s-journey-to-his-first-10k-month-as-a-solo-entrepreneur)
- **Pat Flynn**: no concrete digital-product numbers for 2025–26 were found (gap).
- **Time to first sale**: Printify says most new Etsy shops get a first sale within 30–60 days — [Printify blog](https://printify.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/)
- **Reddit case** (Apr 2026, anecdotal): a printables shop made $4 in its first 2 months. After growing from 11 to 47 listings over about 3 months, it reached about $800/month — [Reddit snapshot](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1sn8suq/snapshots/2026-04-16T21%3A04%3A15.1506Z)
- **Median Etsy shop**: about £100–250/month gross revenue — [Listify AI, Jan 2026](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make-2026). The more optimistic claim of "$100–1,000/month in the first months" and "$1–2k/month for ranking digital shops" is marketing content — [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/how-much-can-you-make-on-etsy/)
- **AI-art experiment**: $27 in 30 days, a net loss after fees — [Goodreads author blog](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/23884190-results-from-selling-ai-art-for-30-days)
- No systematic dataset of Etsy seller incomes exists. Most income claims come from marketing content — [Listify AI](https://www.listifyai.net/blog/how-much-do-etsy-sellers-make-2026)

### Inferences
- Realistic scenario for a full-time employee working about 5–8 h/week, starting Oct 2026 with Etsy plus Payhip or Gumroad and AI help:
  - Month 3: 0–100 €/month.
  - Month 6 (just after Ramadan 2027): 50–300 €/month if the Islamic niche works.
  - Month 12: 100–800 €/month, with a long tail at 0.
  - These ranges are my inference from the median, Printify and Reddit data above, not a measured statistic.
- Passivity ranking: digital downloads on Etsy are semi-passive after setup, with no fulfilment and some customer messages. POD is semi-passive too but adds after-sales issues (defects, delays). Courses and ebooks on Gumroad and Payhip are passive only if the seller already has traffic. Without an audience, Gumroad Discover traffic is weak.
- Gumroad and Payhip have no built-in buyer traffic. Etsy is the only option here with search traffic from day one, so Etsy should be the main channel and Payhip/Gumroad a secondary (cheaper) storefront for social media traffic.

### Gaps
- No verified eRank or EverBee aggregate data on digital-download conversion or income was retrieved.
- No Printful or Printify official seller-income report was found.
- No Pat Flynn data was found.

## 3. Saturation and AI-generated product policies (Etsy, Gumroad, Redbubble)

### Takeaway
Etsy allows AI-assisted digital downloads and POD under its "Creativity Standards", provided they are disclosed ("Designed by" the seller, with AI use stated). Selling prompts alone is banned. Generic digital downloads are saturated: 87 % of listings for "digital downloads" are under $10, and several generic keywords lost 18–71 % of search volume in 2025–26. Niche, specific products are the only realistic way in.

### Cited Findings
- Etsy permits AI-generated or AI-assisted art across digital downloads, physical prints and POD on condition of transparency. Seller-prompted AI art falls under the "Designed by" category — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/ai-image-video/etsy-will-let-you-sell-ai-art-on-its-platform-but-theres-a-catch); [Listadum](https://www.listadum.com/blog/etsy-creativity-standards)
- Selling prompt bundles alone, without a finished artwork, is banned — [Promptless Press](https://www.promptlesspress.com/blog-etsy-ai-policy-2026-digital-products)
- Unverified claim from a metadata-tool vendor: a 10 June 2025 update required "enhanced proof of human involvement" for digital downloads, and non-disclosure risks listing removal or suspension — [AI Metadata Cleaner](https://aimetadatacleaner.com/blog/etsy-ai-disclosure-metadata-guide-2026). Not corroborated by a primary source.
- Saturation signals (RankHero keyword data):
  - "digital download pdf": search volume −44 % (Apr 2025–Mar 2026).
  - "digitaldownload": −18 %, with 23,467 competing listings.
  - "digital download photography": −71 %.
  - 87 % of "digital downloads" listings are priced under $10.
  - Sources: [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-download-pdf); [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digitaldownload); [RankHero](https://www.rankhero.com/keywords/digital-downloads)
- Redbubble moved to account fees in 2025, which penalises low-volume sellers — [Redbubble blog](https://blog.redbubble.com/2025/08/excess-markup-fee-explained/). An analysis describes the decline of Redbubble, Society6 and TeePublic as marketplaces — [JDV Designs](https://jdvdesigns.beehiiv.com/p/decline-print-demand-sites-redbubble-society6-teepublic)

### Inferences
- How Claude can help without breaking the rules:
  - Write planner and journal text content (trackers, du'a pages, reflection prompts, checklists), ebooks and guides.
  - Draft Etsy titles, all 13 tags and descriptions, and FAQs, in both French and English.
  - Generate HTML/SVG or Canva-ready layouts, Notion template structures, and Python scripts that produce PDFs (e.g., reportlab).
- The human role is curation and design in Canva free, plus AI disclosure on Etsy. Keep listings honest; do not mass-upload untouched AI images.
- Saturation is lowest where specificity is high: French-language Islamic products, bilingual FR/AR/EN kids' printables, and "Ramadan 2027" dated planners.

### Gaps
- Etsy's own policy page text (Creativity Standards, AI disclosure field) was not fetched directly.
- Gumroad's AI policy was not checked.
- Redbubble's AI policy (it has restricted AI uploads per account in the past) was not verified for 2026.

## 4. Halal-relevant opportunities: Islamic digital products and Muslim-themed POD, demand and timing for Ramadan 2027

### Takeaway
Ramadan 2027 is expected to start about Mon 8 Feb 2027 (some countries 9 Feb), with Eid al-Fitr about 9–10 March 2027. Etsy has an active but fragmented niche: Ramadan planners, kids' Ramadan calendars and activity books, and Eid cards, priced low (about $3–10). Visible shop volumes are modest (e.g., 141 sales since 2024 for one specialised shop). Hard market-size data for francophone Muslim digital products was not found.

### Cited Findings
- Ramadan 2027 is projected to start on 8 Feb 2027, with the crescent sighting on the evening of 7 Feb — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/ramadan/when-is-ramadan-2027-the-expected-date-moon-sighting-and-eid-explained-1.500630523); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/09/02/expected-ramadan-2027-start-date-and-eid-al-fitr-holiday-dates-in-uae/)
- Eid al-Fitr is projected for 9 March 2027 by [Wego](https://blog.wego.com/ramadan-2027/) and 10 March 2027 by [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/ramadan/when-does-ramadan-2027-begin-everything-you-need-to-know-1.500630523). The outlier estimate of 5 Feb from [ConvertTime](https://converttime.com/en/blog/when-is-ramadan-2027) is less reliable. The user's window of "8 Feb – 9 Mar 2027" is consistent with these sources.
- Etsy examples:
  - The EidCollective shop shows "141 sales since 2024", rated 4.9 — [Etsy EidCollective](https://www.etsy.com/shop/EidCollective)
  - A 30-day Ramadan activity book for kids at $10, with reviews praising its bilingual EN/Turkish version and a Ramadan certificate — [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1880174811)
  - Planner bundles (monthly calendar, weekly tracker, daily journal, plus Eid cards) are common, and physical personalised 3D Ramadan calendars sell at about $21.57 — [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1667513504); [Etsy listing](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1382377938)
- Iftar and suhoor printables have had editorial coverage (2019, older) — [Amaliah](https://amaliah.com/post/55501/6-iftar-suhoor-ramadan-printables-etsy)
- Ramadan spending:
  - Muslim halal lifestyle spending grows annually, with Ramadan as the main catalyst (citing DinarStandard SGIE 2023) — [JIEP paper](https://jiep.uinkhas.ac.id/index.php/JIEP/article/download/340/140/1498)
  - Online transactions in MENA reportedly +23 % in Ramadan 2024 (LinkedIn, weak source) — [LinkedIn](https://in.linkedin.com/in/umarzaz)
  - Shoppers start buying 2–3 weeks before Ramadan — [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/trending-ramadan-banners-2025)

### Inferences
- Suggested timeline for Ramadan 2027, inferred by applying eRank's 8–12-week lead-time rule (see section 5):
  - Mid-Nov to mid-Dec 2026: have Ramadan planners, kids' calendars, Quran-reading trackers (khatm, 30 juz), and Eid card or tag printables live, so Etsy has time to index them and gather early sales.
  - Mid-January to 8 Feb 2027: buyer peak for planners and calendars.
  - About 1–2 weeks before 9–10 March: peak for Eid cards, gift tags and Eid kids' activity sheets.
  - After Eid: Hajj and Dhul-Hijjah products (around mid-May 2027 — date not verified).
- The French-language angle (planner Ramadan en français, calendrier Ramadan enfant, cartes Aïd Moubarak) is likely less competitive than English, but its search volume is unverified.
- POD (Muslim-themed apparel such as "Ramadan Mubarak" or calligraphy tees and tote bags) needs about 1–2 weeks of production and shipping, so list by early January.
- Halal content filters:
  - Avoid gambling and alcohol themes, adult content, and riba-based products (e.g., credit-card or loan-interest trackers).
  - Budget planners should frame debt-free or zakat calculations carefully. A zakat calculator should cite a recognised fiqh basis or recommend checking with a scholar.
  - Images of living beings: many scholars discourage or prohibit depicting animate beings (humans, animals) in images; others allow non-realistic or children's educational use, or faceless styles. Many Islamic Etsy sellers use faceless characters, geometry, calligraphy, lanterns and crescents as a neutral solution. This is general knowledge stated neutrally; no source was fetched.
  - Accuracy of Quranic text and Arabic calligraphy is critical. Errors in āyāt are a serious issue, so AI-generated Arabic must be checked by a human.

## 5. Winter seasonality: Black Friday, Christmas, New Year planners — what sells and when to list

### Takeaway
eRank says Q4 holiday planning must start before November, because competitors are already listed and buyers are already searching. Digital downloads have an advantage as instant last-minute purchases (printables, planners, e-gift cards) right up to Christmas. 2027 planners and New Year resolution or goal templates have a natural window from Oct to Jan. For a seller starting in mid-Oct 2026, Christmas is largely too late to rank; New Year 2027 planners plus Ramadan 2027 are the realistic first season.

### Cited Findings
- "Holiday planning cannot start in November. By then, shoppers are already searching, competitors are already listing" — [eRank holiday trend reports](https://help.erank.com/blog/holiday-trend-reports-etsy-sellers/)
- Made-to-order items need listing months ahead, while POD products can keep selling much closer to the holiday — [eRank](https://help.erank.com/blog/getting-your-etsy-shop-ready-for-the-top-seasonal-and-holiday-traffic)
- Digital products suit last-minute buyers: promote printables, planners and festive e-gift cards, and add holiday covers or seasonal designs — [eRank last-minute tips](https://help.erank.com/blog/10-tips-to-boost-last-minute-holiday-sales-on-etsy/)
- eRank recommends Easter products be live 8–12 weeks before the holiday — [eRank Easter](https://help.erank.com/blog/plan-for-easter-with-eranks-keyword-tool/)

### Inferences
- Concrete calendar for an Oct 2026 start (my inference from eRank's guidance):
  - By late October: 2027 planners (FR/EN), habit trackers, a "bilan de l'année" (year-in-review) and goal-setting workbook, and budget planners (riba-free framing).
  - Early Nov: Black Friday and Cyber Monday discount via Etsy sales, and bundle pricing on Payhip.
  - Nov–Dec: last-minute printable gifts (gift certificates, winter kids' activity sheets — avoid explicitly Christmas-religious content if that conflicts with the user's principles; neutral "winter" themes are an alternative).
  - Mid-Nov to mid-Dec: Ramadan 2027 products live.
  - Late Dec to mid-Jan: New Year planner peak.
- Whether to sell Christmas-themed products is a personal or fiqh judgement for the user. Neutral winter, New Year planner, and Islamic content avoid the question.
- Year-dated planners lose value after about February. Undated versions extend the selling life.

### Gaps
- No eRank or Etsy numeric data on when "2027 planner" searches peak was retrieved.
- No Black Friday conversion data for digital downloads was found.
