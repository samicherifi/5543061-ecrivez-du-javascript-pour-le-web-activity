## Where can a 0-follower account post a clickable external link?

### Takeaway
At 0 followers the free, direct options are: the **Pinterest** pin link (best), the **Instagram** bio link and Story sticker (except brand-new accounts), the **YouTube** channel or description link reached through a Short's "related video", and Facebook posts within a 2-links-per-month quota. **TikTok** needs either 1,000 followers or a *registered* business account.

### Cited Findings
**TikTok**
- TikTok's support wording, as quoted by a third party: website links are available to accounts with 1,000 or more followers **or** to a Registered Business Account. — [Rocketlink](https://rocketlink.io/blog/how-to-put-link-in-tiktok-bio-without-business-account) [TIERS]
- Some guides only say "switch to a business account" (Settings → Account → Switch to Business Account). Others say verifying the business (legal name, address, sometimes a company ID, 24 to 48 h) is required. **Contradiction between sources.** — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-to-put-a-link-in-tiktok-bio/); [Nestscale](https://nestscale.com/?p=5938); [Outfy 2026](https://www.outfy.com/blog/?p=14299) [TIERS]
- Clickable links can only be added in the mobile app. Below the threshold, a plain-text URL has to be copied by hand. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-to-put-a-link-in-tiktok-bio/) [TIERS]
- The official help page could not be read (navigation only). — [TikTok Support](https://support.tiktok.com/en/using-tiktok/creating-videos/links-on-tiktok)

**Instagram**
- Story link sticker opened to all accounts. New accounts and accounts with repeated guideline violations are excluded. The 10k requirement was removed on 27 Oct 2021. One sticker per slide. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-makes-links-in-stories-available-to-all-accounts/609021/); [Unilink 2026](https://app.unilink.us/blog/instagram-link-sticker-explained) [TIERS]
- Reels and captions: no native clickable link for free. Usual workarounds are "link in bio", Stories, and comment-to-DM. — [U2L 2026](https://u2l.ai/blog/how-to-add-link-to-instagram-post); [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/meta-verified-clickable-links) [TIERS]
- Since Sept 2026, Meta One Advanced ($49.99) and higher tiers can add clickable links to a few organic posts and Reels per month. **Caps conflict**: one source says 4/8/12 Reels for Advanced/Expert/Max. Another says Expert ($149) allows 8 Instagram link reels and 20 Facebook link reels. Caption links were earlier tested for Meta Verified (about 10 per month). — [LBB](https://lbbonline.com/news/Meta-Launches-Paid-Subscription-Offering-for-Users-Creators-and-Brands); [adlibrary](https://adlibrary.com/posts/meta-one-link-caps); [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/meta-verified-clickable-links) [TIERS]

**Facebook**
- Dec 2025: Meta confirmed a test capping organic link posts at 2 per month for professional-mode profiles and Pages without Meta Verified. Publishers are exempt. Affiliate links and comment links were reportedly not affected. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages); [Press Gazette](https://pressgazette.co.uk/news/facebook-tests-charging-creators-and-businesses-to-post-more-than-two-links-per-month/)
- 17 Sept 2026: the test was widened ("two link posts on Facebook per month" unless the account pays for Meta One). The article does not list countries, and it does not say whether comment or affiliate links count. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-pages-get-charged-for-link-posts/)
- Blotato says the current rules **do count** comment links, which **contradicts** the 2025 reports. — [Blotato](https://www.blotato.com/blog/meta-one-link-limits) [TIERS]

**YouTube Shorts**
- Links in Shorts descriptions and comments have not been clickable since 31 Aug 2023, for both creators and commenters (anti-spam measure). Prominent clickable links were added to the channel page instead. — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2023/08/10/youtube-shorts-comments-spam/) (2023, but still in force according to [link.boo 2026](https://link.boo/guides/youtube-shorts-link-in-description))
- Workarounds: a long-form video description, the channel's "About" links, and the related-video link. — [link.boo](https://link.boo/guides/youtube-shorts-link-in-description); [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/help/?p=1856) [TIERS]

**Pinterest**
- Official community reply: "you can insert it on the destination link of the Pin", with a note about approved affiliate partners and domains. — [Pinterest Business Community](https://community.pinterest.biz/t/could-someone-explain-this-to-me-please/1913)
- One blog says Idea Pins had no outbound links. Not confirmed. Idea Pins have since been merged into standard pins [NON VÉRIFIÉ]. — [Pin Generator](https://pingenerator.com/blog/pinterest-affiliate-links-policy) [TIERS]

**Snapchat**
- July 2023: Snap added a "Website" field to public profiles. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/10/snap-is-teaming-up-with-linktree-to-let-users-include-links-in-their-profiles/)
- April 2024: profile links reportedly removed and reserved for Snap Stars. Links could still be attached to Snaps (paperclip icon). — [Android Authority](https://androidauthority.com/how-to-add-a-link-to-snapchat-public-profile-3345219)
- May 2026: a Spotlight URL sticker is reportedly tappable with a Public Profile. No follower minimum is mentioned. — [Unilink](https://www.unilink.us/blog/snapchat-spotlight-link) [TIERS, link-in-bio vendor]. **Contradicts** Android Authority.

### Inferences
- Pinterest is the only platform where every video can carry its own direct clickable link at 0 followers.
- On TikTok, a registered business account is the only route at 0 followers. The trade-off is limited access to commercial music in the music library (general knowledge, not sourced here).

### Gaps
- Official TikTok, Snap, and Instagram help pages could not be read.
- Threads, LinkedIn, Lemon8, Dailymotion, and Likee/Kwai were not researched (out of time or call budget). X: I found no official source on links at 0 followers.
- I did not check whether the minimum age for links (Story sticker, Meta One) is 18.

## Native shopping and affiliate programs in France, and their thresholds

### Takeaway
**YouTube Shopping (launched in France on 13 Oct 2026, YPP + 500 subscribers)** and **TikTok Shop affiliate (France since March 2025, reportedly 1,000 to 1,500 followers + age 18)** are the two native programs. **Neither is reachable at 0 followers.** For Instagram and Facebook, I found no native creator affiliate program confirmed for France.

### Cited Findings
- YouTube Shopping launched in France on 13 Oct 2026. Creators can tag products in videos, Shorts, and lives. AI can tag products automatically. Amazon is named. YouTube claims up to 110% more clicks than description links. — [Google Blog FR (OFFICIEL)](https://blog.google/intl/fr-fr/nouveautes-produits/lunivers-youtube/youtube-shopping-france/)
- Criteria: YPP membership, **500 subscribers** (lowered at the end of March 2026), channel not made for kids, no strikes. Up to 60 products per long-form video. Commission varies by merchant. — [Blog du Modérateur](https://www.blogdumoderateur.com/youtube-shopping-france-fonctionnement-criteres-eligibilite/); [Les Gens d'Internet, Mar 2026](https://gensdinternet.fr/2026/03/27/youtube-shopping-devient-accessible-a-davantage-de-createurs/amp/); [Storyboard18](https://storyboard18.com/digital/youtube-opens-shopping-affiliate-program-to-creators-with-500-subscribers-93649.htm)
- **Contradiction:** QUASA says French creators *cannot* join the native affiliate program ("les liens restent ouverts, pas l'affiliation native"). That article probably predates 13 Oct 2026. — [QUASA](https://quasa.io/fr/media/youtube-shopping-en-france-les-liens-restent-ouverts-pas-l-affiliation-native)
- Note: the official Google blog does not say "YPP + 500" outright. It only mentions "easing the eligibility criteria". — [Google Blog FR](https://blog.google/intl/fr-fr/nouveautes-produits/lunivers-youtube/youtube-shopping-france/)
- TikTok Shop has been live in France since 31 March 2025. Since 2026, creators in France, Germany, Spain, and Italy can apply from their profile (no thresholds stated). — [ValueYourNetwork, Aug 2026](https://www.valueyournetwork.com/en/tiktok-shop-creators-in-france/) [TIERS]
- TikTok Shop France affiliate thresholds as reported: 1,500 followers (a creator who says he saw it in his invitation), 1,000 (another guide), or a range of 1,000 to 5,000 depending on the country. Age 18+. Pro account. No minimum views. — [Lucas Fonseque 2026](https://lucasfonseque.fr/debloquer-tiktok-shop-france-2026-guide-createur/); [Formalive](https://www.formalive.fr/affiliation-tiktok-shop/); [Shopify FR](https://www.shopify.com/fr/blog/affiliation-tiktok) [TIERS, **contradictory**]
- Instagram: the creator marketplace (paid partnerships, not affiliate) was extended to France in May 2024. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/13/instagram-expands-its-creator-marketplace-to-10-new-countries)
- Instagram Shopping tags for merchants in France date from 2019. Creator tagging requires an approved merchant with a catalog. — [FashionNetwork 2019](https://au.fashionnetwork.com/news/Instagram-Shopping-deploys-in-eight-countries-outside-the-USA,959461.html) [NON VÉRIFIÉ, >2025]; [Racklify](https://racklify.com/encyclopedia/what-is-an-instagram-creator-product-tag/)

### Inferences
- For someone starting from 0, the fastest native path is YouTube: reach 500 subscribers **and** YPP membership. It is unclear whether YPP at the 500 level (the expanded YPP, with watch-hour requirements) counts. Google lists France as a country where the expanded YPP is fully rolled out. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/troubleshooter/13536952)

### Gaps
- No official TikTok Shop FR page with the affiliate threshold.
- Exact YouTube Shopping commission rates and merchants in France (other than Amazon) are not public.
- Pinterest and Snap have no native affiliate program found for France.

## Rules on affiliate links, shorteners, and cloaking

### Takeaway
I found no explicit ban on affiliate links on any of the platforms covered. Pinterest is the most explicit: deceptive redirects and abused shorteners are banned ("no surprises" principle). Disclosure is still required (legal obligation, see the inferences).

### Cited Findings
- Pinterest: "Don't link to websites that are unsafe, deceptive…". Shorteners "abused for spam, may be blocked". Don't use redirection to reach a site that is not allowed. Don't redirect existing Pins to new destinations. Monetization must not be "misleading" or "repetitive". — [Pinterest Community Guidelines (OFFICIEL)](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/community-guidelines)
- Pinterest: redirect chains through 3 or 4 domains can get pins removed. Disclose ("affiliate link") near the top of the description. — [Pin Generator](https://pingenerator.com/blog/pinterest-affiliate-links-policy) [TIERS]
- Facebook: the 2025 test still allowed affiliate links. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages)

### Inferences
- In France, the "influencers" law (loi n° 2023-451 du 9 juin 2023) requires a clear "Publicité" or "Collaboration commerciale" label for affiliate content [NON VÉRIFIÉ in this session: general knowledge].
- Shortened links: these are risky on Pinterest. On the other platforms I found no official text. Prefer a transparent domain (your own link-in-bio page).

### Gaps
- Official TikTok, Meta, YouTube, and Snap texts on affiliate links and shorteners were not reviewed.

## Algorithmic penalties for external links

### Takeaway
Penalties are **disputed and poorly documented**. X denies them (Oct 2025). Facebook says it does not penalize links but reach is low. On Facebook and Instagram the limit is now **structural** (paid quota) rather than algorithmic.

### Cited Findings
- X: in Oct 2025, Nikita Bier (head of product) denied any downranking of link posts and blamed user behaviour. Musk had previously said the opposite (off-platform links get less boost). — [roboin.io](https://roboin.io/article/en/2025/10/20/x-product-chief-denies-rumor-that-link-posts-are-deboosted/); [tech-ish](https://tech-ish.com/?p=69515); [SAN](https://san.com/cc/even-our-tech-overlords-dont-know-how-x-works-is-there-hope-for-the-rest-of-us/)
- Facebook: no official penalty, but more than 95% of feed posts contain no link, and organic reach for Pages has fallen. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/heres-where-each-of-the-big-social-platform-stands-on-external-links/733946/)
- YouTube: there is no "penalty" as such. Links in Shorts are simply disabled (anti-spam, 2023). — [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2023/08/10/youtube-shorts-comments-spam/)

### Gaps
- No reliable data on a TikTok or Pinterest penalty for affiliate links. Threads and LinkedIn were not researched.
